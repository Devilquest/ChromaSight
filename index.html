How to Use ChromaSight
Viewfinder
Reticle

Tap anywhere on the viewfinder to move the reticle. Double-tap the viewfinder, or press the recenter button, to center it.

Freeze / Resume

Freezes the camera frame. Press again to resume live scanning.

Settings
Point (1px)

Measures the color of the single pixel at the center of the reticle.

Smooth (5px)

Measures the average color of a 5×5 pixel area centered on the reticle.

White Balance

Calibrates white balance from the reticle's current target (white or gray), or applies a lighting preset.

Recent Captures
Copy Color

Copies the color value to the clipboard and adds it to Recent Captures.
